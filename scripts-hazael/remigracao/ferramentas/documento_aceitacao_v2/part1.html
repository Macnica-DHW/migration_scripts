1. Summary

1.1 Where the migration stands

The Macnica Americas pages have been migrated to global2 and rebuilt with global2's components; two GWI event pages await a decision (question M6). Their text, images, links, tables, videos and downloads come from the original GWI pages.

99%of the 1,846 links written in the content of the GWI pages compared on 25 Sep are on the global2 pages with the same destination
10links, on 6 pages, whose destination on global2 differs from GWI; each is listed in question M4
0Image or Text with Image components pointing to a missing file or to a file stored on GWI (checked 25 Sep)

What remains is mainly layout refinement: heading levels, spacing and padding, the size of images next to their text, tables, buttons, tabs and lists. These are the topics of Anion's review comments of 21 to 28 September, and most of them concern how the migrated content is laid out.

1.2 Why this document

The same layout topics come up on many pages, and a short comment such as Spacing issues or Padding Issues can be met in more than one way. A written target, with measurements and pictures, lets every remaining page be finished and reviewed the same way by everyone. This document gives that target:

  • Section 3 gives how a page is reviewed and measured: which view, which window width, what it is compared with.
  • Section 4 gives 19 guidelines. Each gives a pass criterion, where it comes from and how to check it, and, where a setting is involved, the global2 setting that produces it. Pictures show accepted and not accepted results wherever the wording could be read in more than one way.
  • Section 5 settles the layout and content points raised in review: what is decided (applied to pages) and what is still open.
  • Section 6 covers how review and page acceptance work.

What this document is for. The guidelines in Section 4 and the decisions in Section 5 are the criteria every page is accepted against; each review comment points to the guideline or decision it concerns.

2. How a migrated page is built

  • P1Content comes from the original GWI page. The text, images, links, tables, videos, downloads, page title and page description of a global2 page are those of its GWI page, in the same order. We do not rewrite, add or remove content; the only differences allowed are listed in G1. Any change to the content of a page is a decision for Macnica Americas (question M1).
  • P2Layout follows GWI, adapted to global2. Each page keeps GWI's arrangement: what sits beside, above or below what. Where GWI's layout cannot be reproduced with global2's components, or where a guideline in Section 4 asks for a different arrangement (for example, an image on the left of its text), the layout differs from GWI and the content stays the same.
  • P3Only global2 components and their settings. Pages are authored in the AEM author with the components and style options that global2 provides; page authors do not edit HTML or CSS. Each guideline names the settings that meet it. Where a component has a fixed space or size that no setting changes, the guideline's numbers allow for it, or list it under Not counted.

3. How a page is reviewed

These conditions apply to every review, so that two reviewers looking at the same page reach the same result.

  • R1View the page as visitors see it (called view mode in this document): add ?wcmmode=disabled to the page address, or use View as Published. Spacing, section links and forms are judged outside the page editor, because the editor adds empty drop zones (about 29px each) that enlarge every gap, section links do not scroll inside it, and form messages appear under the form.
  • R2Desktop: a page area 1,400px wide at zoom 100% (the browser's responsive mode at 1,400 × 900 gives exactly that). Phone: 390 × 844, when the phone view is reviewed (question A9).
  • R3Compare with the original GWI page, opened the same way, side by side. The GWI page has the same address under /content/macnicagwi/americas/mai/en, except that the global2 /solutions pages are under /technology on GWI, GWI names written with capitals are lowercase on global2, with repeated or trailing hyphens reduced to one, and one page was renamed: /products/boards-modules/ibase/embedded-computing/ibase-mi991-mini-itx-motherboard-for-sale is …/embedded-computing/mi991 on GWI. global2 pages use the full window width, so they are wider than GWI by design: compare content and arrangement, not width.
  • R4Open every tab on both pages. Each tab is reviewed as a separate part of the page.
  • R5The site header, navigation, breadcrumb, footer and the buttons fixed to the window (such as Page Top) belong to the site template, not to the page. They are not part of page acceptance: nothing inside them, including their headings and their width, counts for or against a page.
  • R6We ask that each review comment name the page, the block (its title or first words), the guideline it does not meet (for example G8), and the expected result (question J1).
  • R7Measuring. Values are CSS pixels, read with the browser's own inspector (right-click, Inspect) or on a screenshot taken in responsive mode at a device pixel ratio of 1. A space is measured from the lowest visible pixel of the upper block (the bottom of its letters, image, table or button) to the highest visible pixel of the lower block. A block is one component as it appears on the page: a title, a text, an image, a Text with Image, a table, a row of buttons, a video or a list. A grey band edge ends a space: the grey side is judged by G9 (30 to 60px), and the white side by G8. A top-level section is a Container placed directly in the page content, not inside another container (on a page with Sticky Tabs, the sections inside the Sticky Tabs container). Scroll to the bottom of the page once before measuring, so that every image and video has loaded. A value within 2px of a limit passes.
